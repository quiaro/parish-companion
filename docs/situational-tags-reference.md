# Situational Tag Vocabulary Reference

Situational tags capture **what is happening** in a parishioner's life (the circumstance or cause) as distinct from the emotional tag vocabulary, which captures **how it feels**. The relationship between the two is many-to-many by design: one situation (e.g., `divorce_or_separation`) can give rise to several different emotional states depending on the person (loneliness, anger, relief, grief), and one emotional state (e.g., `loneliness`) can stem from several different situations (social isolation, divorce, bereavement, relocation). A verse can be tagged with both an emotional match and a situational match, and a parishioner's message may produce zero, one, or multiple situational tags.

---

## Loss & grief

| Tag                          | Covers                                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| `bereavement`                | Death of a loved one.                                      |
| `pet_loss`                   | Death of a pet.                                            |
| `miscarriage_or_infertility` | Pregnancy loss, or struggles to conceive.                  |
| `terminal_illness`           | Facing a terminal diagnosis — one's own, or a loved one's. |

## Relationships & family

| Tag                      | Covers                                                          |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `marital_conflict`       | Tension or strain within a marriage.                            |
| `divorce_or_separation`  | A marriage ending or partners separating.                       |
| `parenting_struggles`    | Difficulty or strain in raising children.                       |
| `estrangement`           | A broken or severed relationship with family or a close friend. |
| `caregiving`             | Caring for an aging parent, sick spouse, or other dependent.    |
| `new_parenthood`         | The birth or adoption of a child.                               |
| `singleness`             | Navigating life unmarried, or longing for partnership.          |
| `engagement_or_marriage` | An upcoming or recent wedding.                                  |

## Work & finances

| Tag                  | Covers                                             |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `job_loss`           | Being laid off or fired.                           |
| `job_searching`      | Actively looking for work.                         |
| `new_job`            | Starting a new role or position.                   |
| `career_uncertainty` | Uncertainty about vocational direction or calling. |
| `financial_hardship` | Struggling to make ends meet.                      |
| `retirement`         | Transitioning out of the workforce.                |

## Health

| Tag                       | Covers                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `personal_illness`        | One's own physical health struggle.                                                                                                                                                          |
| `mental_health_struggle`  | An ongoing, named mental health condition (e.g., diagnosed depression or anxiety) — distinct from the emotional tags, which capture a feeling in the moment rather than a chronic condition. |
| `addiction_recovery`      | Struggling with, or recovering from, addiction.                                                                                                                                              |
| `chronic_pain_or_illness` | Long-term, ongoing health conditions.                                                                                                                                                        |

## Faith & life direction

| Tag                  | Covers                                                           |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `spiritual_dryness`  | Feeling distant from God, or going through the motions of faith. |
| `discernment`        | Facing a major decision and seeking direction.                   |
| `new_to_faith`       | Recently beginning to explore or practice the faith.             |
| `returning_to_faith` | Returning to the Church after a period away.                     |

## Life transitions & milestones

| Tag                    | Covers                                     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `moving_or_relocation` | Moving to a new home or city.              |
| `graduation`           | Finishing school.                          |
| `aging`                | Facing one's own process of growing older. |
| `empty_nest`           | Children leaving home.                     |

## Crisis & trauma

| Tag                  | Covers                                                                              |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `natural_disaster`   | Experiencing or recovering from a hurricane, wildfire, flood, or similar event.     |
| `accident_or_injury` | A sudden, traumatic physical event — one's own injury or that of a loved one.       |

## Conflict & social

| Tag                             | Covers                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `persecution_or_discrimination` | Facing hostility on account of one's faith or identity.                                                                                                        |
| `betrayal`                      | Being wronged or deceived by someone trusted.                                                                                                                  |
| `social_isolation`              | Lacking community or meaningful social connection — the situational counterpart to the emotional tag `loneliness`, though one does not always imply the other. |
| `bullying_or_harassment`        | Being targeted or mistreated by others.                                                                                                                        |
| `incarceration`                 | Being imprisoned, or navigating life with an incarcerated loved one.                                                                                           |
| `immigration_status`            | Fear, uncertainty, or hardship related to immigration, documentation, or deportation.                                                                          |
