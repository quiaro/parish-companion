# Emotional Tag Vocabulary Reference

## Negative tags (soothed by Bible verse)

| Tag                | Definition                                                                           | Typical phrasing                                                                                                |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sadness`          | A general low, heavy mood, not tied to a specific cause.                             | "I feel so down lately." / "I've just been sad all week."                                                       |
| `grief`            | Sorrow tied specifically to loss or death.                                           | "I lost my mom last month." / "I'm still grieving my dad's passing."                                            |
| `numbness`         | An inability to feel anything, an emotional shutdown.                                | "I don't feel anything." / "I'm just going through the motions."                                                |
| `heartbreak`       | Pain from a relational loss or rejection — a breakup, divorce, or being left.        | "My relationship just ended." / "He left me and I don't know how to move on."                                   |
| `anxiety`          | Diffuse, ongoing worry about the future.                                             | "I can't stop worrying about everything." / "My mind won't quiet down."                                         |
| `fear`             | An immediate or present sense of threat or unsafety.                                 | "I'm scared about what's happening right now." / "I don't feel safe."                                           |
| `dread`            | Anticipatory fear tied to a specific upcoming event.                                 | "I'm terrified about my surgery next week." / "I'm dreading the meeting tomorrow."                              |
| `despair`          | A deep, existential hopelessness — feeling there is no point.                        | "What's even the point anymore?" / "I feel like nothing matters."                                               |
| `hopelessness`     | A sense that there is no path forward, without despair's deeper existential weight.  | "I don't see a way forward." / "Nothing seems like it's going to get better."                                   |
| `loneliness`       | Feeling unseen, unaccompanied, or disconnected from others.                          | "No one really understands me." / "I feel so alone right now."                                                  |
| `rejection`        | Feeling specifically excluded, unwanted, or not chosen.                              | "I wasn't chosen for the job." / "They don't want me around."                                                   |
| `guilt`            | Self-judgment tied to a specific action or behavior.                                 | "I keep messing things up." / "I shouldn't have done that."                                                     |
| `shame`            | Self-judgment tied to identity or self-worth, beyond a single action.                | "I'm so embarrassed by who I am." / "I feel worthless."                                                         |
| `regret`           | Sorrow over a past decision or missed opportunity.                                   | "I wish I'd done things differently." / "I regret not speaking up back then."                                   |
| `anger`            | Strong displeasure or indignation                                                    | "I'm furious about this." / "This isn't fair at all."                                                           |
| `rage`             | Intense, overwhelming anger that overtakes one's ability to reason or stay calm.     | "I'm so angry I can't think straight." / "I completely lost it."                                                |
| `bitterness`       | Anger that has hardened over time, often tied to unforgiveness.                      | "I still can't forgive them." / "I'm still holding onto what they did."                                         |
| `anger_at_god`     | Anger directed specifically at God: feeling betrayed, abandoned, or punished by Him. | "I'm furious at God right now." / "How could God let this happen?"                                              |
| `doubt`            | Uncertainty specifically about faith or belief.                                      | "I don't know if I still believe." / "Where is God in all of this?"                                             |
| `confusion`        | General uncertainty about life direction, not faith-specific.                        | "I don't know what to do." / "I feel completely lost right now."                                                |
| `uncertainty`      | Not knowing what will happen or what to choose in a specific situation or decision.  | "I have no idea what's going to happen." / "I just don't know what the right call is."                          |
| `exhaustion`       | Physical or emotional depletion from sustained effort.                               | "I'm running on empty." / "I can't keep going like this."                                                       |
| `overwhelm`        | Feeling buried by the sheer volume of demands or stress.                             | "Everything is just too much right now." / "I don't know how to handle it all."                                 |
| `discouragement`   | A sense that effort isn't paying off, feeling like giving up.                        | "Nothing I do seems to work out." / "I just want to give up."                                                   |
| `jealousy`         | Resentment over what others have or have achieved.                                   | "I feel resentful of what they have." / "Why does everything come easier for them?"                             |
| `insecurity`       | Doubt about one's own worth or adequacy.                                             | "I always feel like I don't belong here." / "I constantly compare myself to others and feel like I fall short." |
| `helplessness`     | A sense of powerlessness over a specific situation.                                  | "There's nothing I can do about this." / "It's completely out of my hands."                                     |
| `worry_for_others` | Anxiety or fear directed at someone else's wellbeing rather than one's own.          | "I'm scared for my child." / "I can't stop worrying about my spouse."                                           |
| `pride`            | Self-exaltation, vanity, or an unwillingness to admit fault.                         | "I know I'm right and they're wrong." / "I don't need anyone's help with this."                                 |

## Positive tags (amplified by Bible verse)

| Tag                     | Definition                                                                                                 | Typical phrasing                                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `joy`                   | A general felt sense of happiness, not tied to a specific cause or object.                                 | "I'm so happy today." / "Today was such a good day."                                                   |
| `delight`               | A lighter, often momentary version of joy caused by something specific.                                    | "This made me so happy." / "I loved every minute of that."                                             |
| `gratitude`             | A felt sense of thankfulness, often for something received or given.                                       | "I'm so thankful for everything." / "I feel so blessed right now."                                     |
| `hope`                  | Positive anticipation about what's ahead.                                                                  | "I'm really looking forward to what's next." / "Things feel like they're turning around."              |
| `peace`                 | A felt sense of calm and rest, often in the midst of circumstances.                                        | "I feel so calm right now." / "I'm finally at rest about this."                                        |
| `contentment`           | A settled satisfaction with one's current life or circumstances.                                           | "I'm at peace with where I am in life." / "I don't need anything more than this."                      |
| `love`                  | A felt sense of deep affection or relational warmth — either received from others or directed toward them. | "I feel so loved by the people in my life." / "My heart is so full for the people around me."          |
| `courage`               | A felt readiness to face a challenge or difficulty.                                                        | "I feel ready to face this." / "I'm not as scared anymore, I can do this."                             |
| `faith`                 | A felt strength or assurance in one's trust in God.                                                        | "I know God is always there for me." / "I trust God's got this."                                       |
| `wonder`                | Awe or reverence, often in response to something larger than oneself.                                      | "I'm in awe of how God worked this out." / "Creation is just so beautiful, it amazes me."              |
| `relief`                | The release of a burden that has resolved or lifted.                                                       | "That finally worked out, thank God." / "I can finally breathe again."                                 |
| `pride_as_satisfaction` | Healthy pride or satisfaction in an accomplishment, especially one attributed to God's help.               | "I've worked so hard for this, and it finally paid off." / "I can't believe I finished it, thank God." |
